# 🎉 BMPL Platform Updates - Season 2026

Hey everyone! We've got some exciting updates rolling out to the BMPL platform. Here's what's new:

---

## 💰 Automatic 15% Home Team Discount

When you re-sign one of your own players during free agency, you'll now **automatically get a 15% discount** on your winning bid.

---

## 🎨 Smoother Free Agency & Trade Experience

We've updated up the bidding and trade interfaces to hopefully make them easier to use.

---

## 🔄 Seamless Season Transitions

**Behind the scenes, we've made it way easier for the league to move from season to season.**

Here's what this means for you:

**Faster Free Agency Setup**
- The system now automatically knows which players are free agents vs. under contract
- When a new season starts, free agent lists populate instantly
- No more waiting days for player lists to be updated

**Up-to-Date Stats**
- Current season stats automatically feed into the system
- Players without recent MLB stats are automatically filtered out
- You'll only see players who are actually active and signable

**What This Means:**
- ✅ Free agency period can start sooner each season
- ✅ Fewer "ghost players" cluttering the free agent list
- ✅ More accurate budget and roster information
- ✅ Smoother overall experience year-over-year
- ❌ WAR available as a stat on the site
    - As a compromise, we link to bbref pages whenever possible.
